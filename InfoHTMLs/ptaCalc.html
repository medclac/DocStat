Percent Tissue Altered (PTA) Calculator

Calculation ID: ptaCalc
1. Formula & Parameters
PTA = (Ablation Depth + Flap Thickness) / CCT
Normal Range / Target: < 40%

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • CCT: Central Corneal Thickness (µm).
  • Flap: µm.
  • Ablation: µm.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The Percent Tissue Altered (PTA) metric is a validated independent predictor of post-LASIK corneal ectasia [cite: Santhiago MR et al., 2014]. Exceeding a 40% alteration of total corneal thickness severely destabilizes corneal biomechanics, causing progressive bulging and visual loss [cite: Santhiago MR et al., 2014].

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • LASIK Safety Screening
  • Refractive Surgery Planning

Key Medical Decision: Vetos LASIK in high-risk patients, redirecting to PRK or Phakic IOLs.

ParameterNote

4. Pathophysiological Background

Loss of anterior stromal tensile strength bridging the cross-linked collagen lamellae.
