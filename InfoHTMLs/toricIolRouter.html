Algorithmic selection based on prior surgery

  • Clinical Scenario: Toric, Post-Lasik, Post-RK.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

Standard keratometry overestimates corneal power after myopic LASIK and underestimates it after hyperopic LASIK, leading to devastating hyperopic or myopic refractive surprises [cite: ASCRS Post-Refractive Guidelines]. Post-refractive formulas (Barrett True-K, Haigis-L) correct for this altered anterior-to-posterior corneal curvature ratio [cite: ASCRS Post-Refractive Guidelines].

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Cataract Surgery after LASIK/PRK/RK
  • Toric IOL Planning

Key Medical Decision: Identifies the correct regression or true-K formula to prevent refractive surprises.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Adjusts for disrupted Gullstrand's ratio between anterior and posterior corneal surfaces post-ablation.
