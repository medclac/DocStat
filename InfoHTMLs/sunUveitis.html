SUN Uveitis Grading

Calculation ID: sunUveitis
1. Formula & Parameters
Grading based on AC cell count in 1x1 mm beam
Normal Range / Target: Grade 0 (Inactive)

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • AC Cells: Evaluated in a standard slit beam.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The Standardization of Uveitis Nomenclature (SUN) provides a uniform, objective grading scale for anterior chamber inflammation [cite: Jabs DA et al., 2005]. Consistent grading is essential for tracking therapeutic response to topical steroids and determining clinical remission.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Anterior Uveitis / Iritis Management

Key Medical Decision: Titrates frequency of topical corticosteroid drops.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Breakdown of the blood-aqueous barrier allows leukocytes and proteins (flare) into the anterior chamber.
