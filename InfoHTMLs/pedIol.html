1. Formula & Parameters
Age-based hyperopic targeting
Normal Range / Target: Age-appropriate Hyperopia

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Age: Select category for pediatric patients.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

Pediatric eyes grow significantly after cataract surgery (axial elongation), causing a massive myopic shift [cite: Dahan/Enyedi Guidelines]. Surgeons deliberately target significant hyperopia in infants (+6.00 D) to ensure the eye grows into emmetropia by adulthood, preventing severe anisometropic amblyopia.
