Target IOP & VFI Progression

Calculation ID: targetIop
1. Formula & Parameters
Target IOP based on baseline T-Max and severity
Normal Range / Target: Target IOP

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • T-Max: Highest untreated IOP.
  • Severity: Mild, Mod, Severe.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

Establishing a Target Intraocular Pressure (IOP) is the cornerstone of glaucoma management [cite: AAO PPP]. Aggressive percentage reductions (40-50%) are required for severe disease to halt retinal ganglion cell death and preserve visual fields (VFI).

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Primary Open Angle Glaucoma (POAG) Management

Key Medical Decision: Dictates escalation of topical drops, SLT laser, or filtering surgery.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Lowers translaminar pressure gradient to halt mechanical and ischemic optic neuropathy.
