SRK/T IOL Power Formula

Calculation ID: srktIol
1. Formula & Parameters
P = A - 2.5(AL) - 0.9(K)
Normal Range / Target: N/A

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • A-Constant: Manufacturer specific.
  • Axial Length (AL): mm.
  • Average K: Diopters.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The SRK/T formula is a classic third-generation intraocular lens power calculation combining theoretical optics and empirical regression [cite: Retzlaff JA et al., 1990]. It remains remarkably robust for average and long axial lengths, serving as a historical baseline for modern biometric comparisons [cite: Retzlaff JA et al., 1990].

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Cataract Surgery Planning
  • Intraocular Lens (IOL) Power Calculation

Key Medical Decision: Estimates required spherical IOL power to achieve target postoperative refraction.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Models the eye as a simple two-lens optical system (cornea and IOL) with empirically derived adjustments for anterior chamber depth.
