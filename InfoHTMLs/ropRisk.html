ROP Risk (WINROP / G-ROP) & Intravitreal Guide

Calculation ID: ropRisk
1. Formula & Parameters
GA ≤ 30w or BW ≤ 1500g threshold
Normal Range / Target: No Screening Required

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Gestational Age (weeks) & Birth Weight (grams).
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

Retinopathy of Prematurity (ROP) is a leading cause of childhood blindness. Strict screening criteria based on gestational age and birth weight ensure high-risk premature infants receive timely retinal examinations and life-saving laser or anti-VEGF therapy before retinal detachment occurs [cite: AAP/AAO Joint Statement].

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Neonatal Intensive Care (NICU)
  • Retinopathy of Prematurity Screening

Key Medical Decision: Triggers mandatory dilated fundus examinations in the NICU.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Incomplete vascularization of the peripheral retina leading to ischemia and neovascularization.
