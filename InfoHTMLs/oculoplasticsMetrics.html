2. Clinical & Medical Significance

Standardized oculoplastic metrics (MRD1, Hertel values) are critical for objectively diagnosing ptosis and proptosis [cite: AAO Guidelines]. They dictate surgical indications (e.g., levator advancement vs Müller's muscle resection) and track progressive orbital disease.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Ptosis Evaluation
  • Orbital Trauma / Tumors

Key Medical Decision: Guides surgical approach for eyelid reconstruction and orbital volume replacement.
