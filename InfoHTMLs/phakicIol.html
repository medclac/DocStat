Phakic IOL & Muhtaseb Risk

Calculation ID: phakicIol
1. Formula & Parameters
Risk evaluation based on ACD and HWTW
Normal Range / Target: ACD ≥ 2.8mm

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Anterior Chamber Depth (ACD): mm.
  • Horizontal White-to-White (HWTW): mm.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

Improper sizing of phakic IOLs (EVO ICL) leads to severe complications: undersizing causes anterior subcapsular cataract, while oversizing causes angle-closure glaucoma [cite: STAAR Surgical Guidelines]. Accurate vault prediction via ACD and HWTW is essential for surgical safety [cite: STAAR Surgical Guidelines].

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Refractive Surgery (High Myopia)
  • Phakic IOL Sizing

Key Medical Decision: Determines safety of implantation and optimal lens length.
