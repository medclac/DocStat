Vertex Distance Compensation

Calculation ID: vertexDist
1. Formula & Parameters
P_contact = P_spectacle / (1 - (d * P_spectacle))
Normal Range / Target: N/A

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Spectacle Power: Diopters.
  • Vertex Distance: mm.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

As lenses move closer to the eye (from spectacles to contact lenses), minus lenses become more effective and plus lenses become less effective [cite: BCLA Standards]. Vertex distance compensation is mandatory for any prescription exceeding ±4.00 D to avoid over- or under-correction in contact lenses.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Contact Lens Fitting

Key Medical Decision: Converts spectacle Rx to accurate contact lens power.

ParameterNote
Contact PowerRequired for Rx > ±4.00 D [cite: BCLA Standards].
4. Pathophysiological Background

Adjusts for the change in effective focal length based on lens position relative to the principal planes of the eye.
